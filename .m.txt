feat(retention): watchlist with "what moved since your last visit"

Retention work, built around the one mechanic that actually brings people back
to a price-comparison site: "did it drop yet?"

Everything else on the site answers a question the visitor already had. A
watchlist creates a reason to return, and the "since your last visit" framing is
what makes a return visit worth something.

How it works without a backend
- We remember the best price each tracked part had the last time the watchlist
  rendered, and compare it with today's live price on the next visit.
- That is an earned return visit rather than a notification we would need
  accounts, email infrastructure and a consent flow to send.
- Storage is localStorage, like the existing builder picks: no account, no PII,
  no consent. The trade-off is per-device, which is the right call at this stage
  because server-side would mean auth, and auth on a price site is a much
  bigger decision than a watchlist.

A 1% floor on reporting
A drop is only shown when it is at least 1%. A 40 DA move on a 90 000 DA part
is noise, and telling someone "it dropped!" for that trains them to ignore the
badge, which destroys the mechanic. Silence is the correct output when nothing
meaningful moved.

What was added
- lib/watchlist.ts - versioned localStorage store with defensive parsing, plus
  priceMove(), rememberCurrentPrice() and droppedCount(). A corrupt or
  hand-edited payload is discarded rather than allowed to throw: a broken
  watchlist must never take the page down.
- components/WatchButton.tsx - optimistic toggle, reserves its space before
  mount so adding it does not shift the card layout, and re-syncs when another
  card on the page toggles the same part.
- components/WatchlistClient.tsx - the list, with a down/up/unchanged badge, a
  90-day trend bar, and a 90-day-low comparison. Client-side on purpose, since
  the baseline only exists in the visitor's browser; it shows a skeleton rather
  than an empty page so there is no layout shift.
- app/api/watchlist - compact rows for the tracked ids only. The server cannot
  know what is tracked at render time, and shipping the 4 000+ product
  catalogue to filter it in the browser would be wasteful. Capped at 100 ids so
  it cannot be used to request the whole catalogue in one call.
- /watchlist and /fr/watchlist, plus 16 dictionary keys in each language.
- The star is on the product cards in both the card and table layouts.

Verified: tsc clean, next build passes, /api/watchlist returns correct live data
(RTX 4060 at 89 900 DA from Click-DZ, Ryzen 5 5600 at 29 900 DA), both pages
serve, and the button placeholders mount in the category grid. The final
click-through in a browser was not completed: the category page is 1.79 MB and
did not finish parsing within the tooling window, so the toggle is verified by
types, build and mounting placeholders rather than by an observed click. Worth
a manual check on a real device.
